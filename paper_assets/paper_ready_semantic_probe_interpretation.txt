Main interpretation:
The post-merger semantic probe works as a diagnosis tool. Causal patches contain stronger queried-object semantic signal than matched random patches, even when the answer is hallucinated. However, the semantic margin does not reliably separate grounded from hallucinated answers, so it should not be framed as a standalone hallucination detector.
