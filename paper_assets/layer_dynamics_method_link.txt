Layerwise analysis provides additional context for the causal
patch framework. In Qwen 7B, target-aligned patch rankings are weak and less
stable at earlier hidden-state depths, then emerge sharply around the later
half of the model and remain stable through the final state. We treat this as
evidence that the later answer-position representations used by our method
contain a consolidated spatial signal, not as proof of the exact layer at
which the model reads a region.
