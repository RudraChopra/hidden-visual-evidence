As a simple depth baseline, we compare the semantic probe at the
development-selected Qwen 7B hidden-state index with a uniformly chosen
alternative index. We apply the same comparison to the frozen relative-depth
transfer on Qwen 3B. On held-out hallucinations, the selected Qwen 7B depth
produces a mean causal-minus-random target-concept margin of 1.910, compared
with 0.408 across alternative layers. The corresponding Qwen 3B values are
1.506 and 0.467. In both models, the selected depth exceeds every alternative
layer in mean margin (exact layer-randomization p=0.034 and p=0.027).
This is a lightweight baseline rather than a new model-selection claim.
